Tier 4 progression setup

Recommended order: item level → daily content → weekly/limited sources → resource-specific exchanges.

1

Item Level

Press P → Character Profile. 1640–1729 is Lower Tier 4. At 1730+, Upper Tier 4 becomes relevant; Upper honing materials are used on Destined Tremor gear after that gear piece is upper-transferred.

2

Complete daily content

Highest available Kurzan Front / Chaos content and Guardian content are the daily core. Procyon’s Compass lists scheduled Field Boss, Chaos Gate and Adventure Island rewards.

3

Claim weekly and limited sources

Weekly/limited sources: Guild Bloodstone, Growth Boost, Clear Medal, Milestone Weekly Missions, raid exchanges and active event shops. Haal’s Hourglass is added at 1730+.

4

Spend for the current character

Materials must match the character’s current gear and unfinished progression systems. Old-tier stock and completed systems no longer add progression value.
