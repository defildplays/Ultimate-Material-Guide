Paradise — weekly priority

One of your largest repeatable weekly progression sources.

Run Paradise every week

Season 4 is active through February 3, 2027. Paradise is per-character and feeds itself: Elysian builds your Paradise gear, Crucible earns keys, Hell turns those keys into progression rewards.

WED 10:00 UTCPER CHARACTER
1
Elysian

Use your weekly entries. Farm Legacy of Paradise gear plus Restoration / Resonance materials. Better Paradise gear helps you push Crucible.

2
Crucible

Push the highest boss you can each week. Your weekly placement awards Hell Keys; every participant who clears gets keys, while higher placement gives more / better keys.

3
Hell

Spend your Hell Keys. Reward chests can contain Leapstones, Fusion Materials, Shards, Honing Support Materials, gold and other progression items. Key rewards follow your Paradise item-level bracket.

Where: Alt+Q → Core Content → Paradise
Also check: Paradise Material Exchange for extra Elysian / Crucible entries and Hell Keys.

Upper-transfer Destined Hellfire Gear into Destined Tremor Gear. The transfer requires eligible 1730+ gear with Advanced Honing +40. After the transfer, stop treating Lower T4 stones/leapstones/fusion mats as your main honing set and switch to the 1730→1800 tab.

Paradise Season 4

Season 4 runs through February 3, 2027. At 1730+, Hell Keys were updated to give Upper T4 honing rewards, so Paradise should be part of your normal weekly route.

3

Before reset: use your entries, push Crucible, then spend your Hell Keys.

Active on October 1, 2026. These are real progression sources right now.

Maharaka Returns

September 16, 2026 → January 20, 2027 · open the Maharaka Play Map beside the minimap.

DAILY + WEEKLY + SHOP

Daily — 3 minigames

Complete 3 Maharaka missions each day for Maharaka Postcards. The minigames can be replayed, but the efficient resource loop is the 3 daily missions. Daily event missions follow the normal daily reset.

Weekly — stamp track

Each completed mission gives a stamp. Every 3 stamps gives 500 Postcards, with up to 9 stamps per week in the event format. Finish the weekly stamps before Wednesday reset.

Shop — spend both currencies

Spend Maharaka Postcards in the event shop, and decide what to convert into Limited Maharaka Postcards for the second reward pool. Buy weekly-limited honing stock before reset.

Extra while you are there: check Gathering Wishes for accumulated free reward boxes, use the free event feast, and use Viking Paradise's direct portals to the Field Boss and Chaos Gate locations.
